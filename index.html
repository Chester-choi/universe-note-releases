About Universe Note

Universe Note is a note-taking app for Mac, iPhone and iPad that keeps your work notes, calendar and tasks on one screen. Notes are stored only on your devices and in your own iCloud. There is no sign-up and no ads.

  • View and edit the calendars and reminders already on your device
  • Connect Google Calendar, Outlook and CalDAV calendars directly in the app (Universe Note Pro)
  • Create a meeting note from any event in one tap
  • iCloud sync across your Mac, iPhone and iPad, plus automatic backups

Google Calendar

When you connect a Google account, Universe Note reads your calendar list and events so it can show them in the day, week, month and year views, and it saves the events you create, edit or delete back to Google Calendar. The app talks to Google directly from your device. Your Google data is not sent to the developer's servers, is not shared with anyone, and is not used for advertising or to train AI models. See the privacy policy for details.

Universe Note's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
